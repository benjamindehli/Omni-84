Omni-84 is a complete sampling of the Suzuki Omnichord OM-84. Every sound the instrument makes was recorded into individual samples: the drum machine, the bass, all 84 chords across seven chord types, the monophonic keyboard voice and both SonicStrings voices.

Seven modes are switched from inside the interface:

- Bass.
- Chords.
- Chords (Looped).
- Drums.
- Keyboard.
- SonicStrings.
- SonicStrings (AutoStrum).

AutoStrum works the way the instrument does: the chord keys choose the chord, two dedicated keys perform the ascending and descending strum, and changing chord while notes are still ringing morphs the sounding notes across rather than restarting the strum. Strum speed runs free or locks to the host tempo, snapping to note values from 1/4 to 1/32 including dotted and triplet.

The Space control is a convolution reverb built from the chain the real instrument was played through: a Roland PA-120 mixer preamp and its spring reverb, a Fulltone Tube Tape Echo in the effects loop, and a Roland Dimension D SDD-320 for stereo width.

277 samples at 48 kHz / 24-bit, stored as lossless FLAC in one sample file that the installer puts in place. Only the active mode is decoded into RAM.

## Features

- Seven modes: Bass, Chords, Chords (Looped), Drums, Keyboard, SonicStrings, SonicStrings (AutoStrum).
- All 84 chords: major, minor, 7th, minor 7th, major 7th, diminished and augmented.
- AutoStrum with a select-and-strum key layout and chord-change morphing, including release tails.
- Tempo-synced strum speed from the host, a manual BPM or free-running, snapping to 1/4 through 1/32 with dotted and triplet values.
- Space convolution reverb from a Roland PA-120, a Fulltone Tube Tape Echo and a Dimension D SDD-320.
- Lowpass filter, monophonic or polyphonic keyboard voice, sustain shaping on SonicStrings.
- Drum mode maps kick, snare, hi-hat and clave with labels above the keyboard.
- Looped chord mode for notes held longer than eleven seconds.
- 277 samples at 48 kHz / 24-bit, stored losslessly (241 MB source library).
